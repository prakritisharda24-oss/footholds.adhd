## About

**Footholds: Tiny Wins for Busy Brains**

Footholds is an AI-powered habit tracker designed specifically for adults with ADHD. Mainstream habit apps rely on rigid streaks and all-or-nothing goals, which punish the inconsistency that comes with ADHD and lead many users to abandon them within weeks. Footholds is built on the opposite principle: showing up matters more than perfection.

Every habit includes a "tiny win," the smallest version that still counts on a low-energy day, such as two sips of water instead of a full glass. Users check in with their "brain battery" each day, and the app reorders their list to match, nudging them toward tiny wins when energy is low. Progress is visible and rewarding, with a daily battery that fills up, celebratory animations, points, levels, and unlockable color themes.

Artificial intelligence sits at the core of the experience. New users answer three playful questions and receive a personalized starter plan. An AI "brain buddy" breaks intimidating habits into two-minute steps, helps users get unstuck, and delivers a weekly, shame-free review of their patterns. A visual focus timer addresses time blindness, while a live focus room supports body doubling, letting users work alongside others in real time. Habits can also be added to Google Calendar as recurring reminders.

Footholds follows a freemium business model. The free tier covers the essentials, while Footholds Plus, at $4.99 per month, unlocks unlimited habits and AI coaching, "oops passes" that protect streaks on missed days, and personal insights into when users follow through best.

A built-in founder dashboard tracks anonymous, aggregated metrics including retention, activation, feature adoption, and conversion, alongside an interactive unit-economics model covering revenue, AI costs, and customer payback. Together, these features demonstrate how AI can deliver both meaningful user value and a sustainable business.
